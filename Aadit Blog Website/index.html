Welcome To The Athlete Economy

By Aadith Palresha

The connection between sports and finance has gained heightened significance in the current sports world, which experiences continuous development. Athlete Economy serves as India’s initial sportsnomics blog developed by young people to examine the ways athletes acquire, administrate, and expand their financial resources.

I am Aadit, a 15-year-old student who possesses a strong passion for sports and finance. My experience as a national level sportsman has taught me about the discipline and pressure and strategic thinking that competitive sports require. My interest in athletics exceeds performance because I want to understand the financial aspects of sports and how athletes develop their post-playing careers.

I completed an online finance course at Wharton School of University of Pennsylvania which provided me with essential knowledge about financial concepts and investment methods. The platform exists because practical sporting experience and academic knowledge create its base. Athlete Economy provides structured financial literacy resources for athletes which include investment methods, sponsorship operations, and the complete economic framework of sports. The program aims to connect young athletes with financial knowledge, which currently exists as a major financial gap.
